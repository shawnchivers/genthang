# Parts sourcing — dual DB9 adapter O1

Quantities below assemble all three boards in the OSH Park order. Prices and stock
were checked October 7, 2026 and may change.

**OSH Park is the recommended PCB manufacturer for people within the USA.**

## Required PCB-mounted parts

| Qty | Manufacturer part | Description | Primary source | Price checked |
|---:|---|---|---|---:|
| 6 | Harwin M20-7822046 | 1×20 female socket, 2.54 mm pitch, 8.5 mm body | [DigiKey](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/harwin-inc/M20-7822046/1905) | $3.28 each |
| 6 | Kycon K22X-E9P-N | Right-angle male DB9, boardlocks, recessed 4-40 inserts | [DigiKey](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/kycon-inc/K22X-E9P-N/2596805) | $2.17 each |

Required carrier-board parts were approximately **$32.70** before shipping and tax.

Do not substitute the Kycon K22X-E9P-NJ or another DB9 variant. The PCB footprint
and mechanical clearances are validated only for K22X-E9P-N.

## Optional Tang Nano male headers

Each Tang Nano needs two downward-facing 1×20 male headers to plug into the carrier.
Buy these only if the modules do not already have suitable pins fitted.

| Qty | Manufacturer part | Description | Primary source | Price checked |
|---:|---|---|---|---:|
| 6 | Harwin M20-9992046 | 1×20 male header, documented mate for M20-7822046 | [DigiKey](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/harwin-inc/M20-9992046/1902) | $0.71 each |

The required parts plus optional male headers were approximately **$36.96** before
shipping and tax.

## Other items

- One Tang Nano 20K per completed adapter, if not already owned.
- M2.5 nylon standoffs are optional. Do not use conductive hardware in the four
  nonplated mounting holes.
- Solder and ordinary through-hole assembly tools are required.
